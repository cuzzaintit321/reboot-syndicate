WHAT CHANGES
THE PRICE?

Website size and content. The number of pages, copywriting, photography, custom functionality and work needed to migrate an existing website affect the estimate.

Integrations and ongoing care. Booking tools, store functionality, hosting, domain fees, paid software and maintenance should each be identified in your quote. Do not assume every ongoing cost is included in a build fee.

Campaign costs. Advertising management and the budget paid to Google or Meta are different expenses. Confirm both before starting a campaign.

Deliverables and ownership. Compare the actual page list, revision scope, account access, handoff and reporting when evaluating proposals. A lower headline price may cover a different amount of work.

BEFORE YOU COMMIT.

What should I send to get a useful quote?

Send your website if you have one, the services you sell, your service area, the action visitors should take, and your preferred timing and budget. Mention any booking, payment or CRM tools you need connected.

Should I start with a website, SEO or advertising?

If visitors cannot understand your offer or contact you easily, start with the website. If a useful website already exists, assess search visibility and tracking before selecting ongoing SEO or paid campaigns.

Does paying for SEO guarantee a top ranking?

No. The work can improve technical accessibility and relevance, but search placement depends on competition and factors outside an agency's control. Agree on deliverables and measure qualified inquiries alongside search visibility.

Are these prices a comparison with other Brevard agencies?

No. These are Reboot Syndicate's own starting prices. To compare providers, request written quotes for the same scope.
