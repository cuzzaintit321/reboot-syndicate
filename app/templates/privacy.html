Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026. Reboot Syndicate respects the privacy of people visiting this website and contacting us.

When you submit a consultation request, we collect the name, email address, optional phone number, selected service, and project description you provide. We use inquiry details to respond to your request, discuss services, manage our business communications, and protect the website from abuse. Inquiry records are stored in our business application and may be sent through an email delivery provider to notify us of new requests.

Service providers and links

Hosting, email, payment, or analytics services may process limited information as needed to deliver their functionality. Linked third-party websites have their own privacy practices. We do not publish consultation submissions on the website.

Cookies and analytics

Some website features or third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies. The technologies in use can change as services are added or removed; please contact us for details about a specific service.

Retention and choices

We retain inquiry records for business, security, and legal needs. You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information by contacting us. Some records may need to be retained where required by law or legitimate business obligations.
